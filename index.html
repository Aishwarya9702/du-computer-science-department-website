About the Department

The Department of Computer Science at the University of Delhi is dedicated to providing quality education and encouraging research in computer science and related fields. The department focuses on developing strong foundations in programming, algorithms, data structures, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. Through academic learning, practical experience, and research activities, the department aims to equip students with technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and innovative thinking to prepare them for careers in academia, research, and the technology industry.

Department Gallery

Department History

The Department of Computer Science at the University of Delhi was established in 1981 with the aim of providing quality education in computer science. Over the years, the department has expanded its academic programmes and research activities, contributing to the development of skilled professionals and researchers. The department continues to promote innovation, technical excellence, and advanced learning in emerging areas of computing.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized centre of excellence in computer science education and research by promoting innovation, creativity, ethical leadership, and technological advancement for the benefit of society.

Our Mission

  • To provide high-quality education in fundamental and advanced computer science.
  • To encourage innovative research and technological development.
  • To develop analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and creativity among students.
  • To prepare students for careers in academia, research, and industry.

Academic Programmes

The Department of Computer Science offers postgraduate and research programmes and contributes to undergraduate computer science education across the University of Delhi.

Undergraduate Programmes

B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science

An undergraduate programme focusing on programming, algorithms, data structures, databases, software engineering, and emerging technologies.

B.Sc. Programme with Computer Science

A multidisciplinary undergraduate programme combining computer science with other academic disciplines.

Postgraduate Programmes

M.Sc. Computer Science

A postgraduate programme covering advanced computing concepts, artificial intelligence, data science, algorithms, and research methodologies.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

A professional postgraduate programme emphasizing software development, application engineering, databases, and computer systems.

Doctoral Programme

Ph.D. in Computer Science

A research-oriented doctoral programme focused on advanced theoretical and applied computer science.

Research opportunities include artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, information security, and interdisciplinary computing.

Research Areas

The Department of Computer Science encourages research in fundamental, applied, and emerging areas of computing.

Artificial Intelligence

Intelligent systems, automated reasoning, and computational problem-solving.

Machine Learning

Predictive modelling, deep learning, and data-driven algorithms.

Natural Language Processing

Text analysis, sentiment analysis, language models, and information extraction.

Data Science

Data mining, big data analytics, and knowledge discovery.

Cybersecurity

Information security, cryptography, and secure computing systems.

Computer Vision

Image processing, pattern recognition, and visual data analysis.

Software Engineering

Software architecture, development methodologies, and system design.

Computer Networks

Network communication, distributed systems, and Internet of Things.
